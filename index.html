How it works

Three steps from finished job to sent quote

  1. 1

    The sheet goes in the tray

    The office types in the job sheet. If the materials or hours are missing, it still goes in, flagged so the office can chase it.

    Office · about 1 minute
  2. 2

    You price it on your phone

    Oldest job first. Materials, labour and VAT add up as you go. Don't agree with the sum? Type your own price and say why.

    Owner · under 2 minutes
  3. 3

    Approve, and the office sends it

    One clear approve button with a confirm. Nothing reaches the customer until the office sends it, and every step is logged.

    Office · same day

Your prices, your call

It does the sums. You make the decision.

Trayclear never prices, approves or sends anything by itself. It just gets the right job in front of you at the right time.

Try the demo Opens with nine sample jobs. Try the 2-minute challenge.
  • No automatic pricing. The calculated figure is a starting point, never the answer.
  • Override with a reason. Old wiring on a farm job? Set a fair price and note why.
  • Approved means locked. The office can't change a price you've signed off.
  • Fewer taps than paper. One tap opens a job, one tap approves it.

Does Trayclear set my prices?

No. It adds up what's on the job sheet and shows you a starting figure. You can accept it, change any line, or type your own price. Nothing is approved or sent without you.

Do my lads need to learn a new app?

Not to start. The office types in the paper sheets as they come back. Capturing hours and materials on site is planned next, kept to as few taps as possible.

Is the demo real?

The demo works fully, but the firm, the customers and every figure in it are made up. It saves only in your own browser, and "Reset demo" puts everything back.
